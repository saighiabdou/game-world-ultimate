Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Important: GAME WORLD ULTIMATE is currently a prototype, not a live store.

1. Information collected

This prototype does not ask visitors to enter names, email addresses, passwords, game account credentials or payment details. The demo shopping cart exists temporarily in the current page and is not connected to a real order system.

2. Payments and accounts

User registration, real payments, seller payouts and automatic game top-ups are not active. Do not enter payment details or sensitive information.

3. Cookies and analytics

The current prototype does not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics trackers. The hosting provider may process technical access and security logs under its own policies.

4. Privacy and deletion

There are no user accounts or saved profiles in this prototype. Account deletion, profile visibility settings and marketing preferences are therefore not available. Reloading or closing the page clears the temporary demo cart.

5. Future changes

This policy must be updated if real accounts, payments, analytics or other data processing features are introduced.

6. Contact

A public support contact has not yet been configured.

5. Modifications futures

Cette politique devra être mise à jour si des comptes réels, paiements, analyses ou autres traitements de données sont ajoutés.

6. Contact

Aucun contact public d'assistance n'a encore été configuré.
